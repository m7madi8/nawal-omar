How we hold this experience

The day

Day program

  1. 09:30

    Arrival & welcome + opening circle

    Welcome with light healthy hospitality, coffee, tea, and a calm space to settle in. A warm women’s circle to meet, break the ice, and set the intention you’ll carry through the day.

  2. Yoga + breath

    Yoga + breathwork (Wim Hof inspired)

    A deep yoga session for all levels, woven with breath techniques inspired by the Wim Hof Method.

    • Calm the nervous system.
    • Build focus, energy, and mental readiness for the ice bath.

  3. Ice Bath

    Ice Bath experience

    A guided, considered experience — not about challenging the body, but discovering the strength already within you.

    • Strengthen the nervous system.
    • Improve how you meet stress and pressure.
    • Increase focus and mental clarity.
    • Ease inflammation and muscle tension.
    • Grow self-trust by stepping beyond the comfort zone.

  4. Sauna

    Sauna + contrast

    After the ice bath, we move into the sauna to receive the benefits of heat–cold contrast.

    • Improve circulation.
    • Relax the muscles.
    • Support recovery.
    • Leave body and mind with a deep sense of ease.

  5. Lunch

    Lunch break

    A gourmet, healthy lunch with free time to enjoy the space, talk, and connect with the other women.

  6. Workshop

    Natural flower crown workshop

    Each woman creates a natural flower crown by hand — a beautiful keepsake to take home after the day.

Not suitable for

For your safety, we do not recommend ice bath participation if you have:

  • Pregnancy.
  • Unstable heart disease or angina.
  • Uncontrolled high blood pressure.
  • Epilepsy.
  • Serious circulatory disorders.
  • Severe Raynaud’s disease.
  • A recent heart attack or stroke.
  • Any condition where a doctor advises against extreme cold exposure.

If you have any health condition or take ongoing medication, please consult your doctor before registering.

Why

Why this day is worth giving yourself

  1. You’ll leave with tools that stay with you

    Breathwork and nervous-system regulation aren’t for one day only — they’re skills you can use in daily life.

  2. You’ll learn to meet pressure with calm

    The ice bath teaches that inner calm is stronger than any outer circumstance.

  3. You’ll feel the power of women’s community

    When women gather in a safe space free from comparison and judgment, something beautiful happens — we remember we’re not alone, and real support opens new doors within us.

  4. You’ll give yourself a pause

    Away from the phone, work, responsibilities, and constant running — so you remember what peace feels like when you put yourself first.

  5. Real investment starts with you

    Every time you choose yourself, you’re not escaping life — you’re returning to it with clearer energy, a lighter heart, and a more present body.
